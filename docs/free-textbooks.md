# Free / open ML textbooks (reference corpus)

The textbooks the figure-recreation work drew on. Each is genuinely free to read; we rebuilt the
*architectures and concepts* (public facts) in mlatlas's own style and did **not** reuse any book's
figures, so the figure licenses below are informational. "OPEN" = figures are themselves reusably
licensed; "read-only" = free to read but figures are copyrighted (we redraw from scratch).

24 books.

## 1. Dive into Deep Learning (D2L)
- **Authors:** Aston Zhang, Zachary C. Lipton, Mu Li, Alexander J. Smola
- **Free access:** Fully free interactive HTML, per-chapter PDF and notebooks at d2l.ai; source at github.com/d2l-ai/d2l-en
- **Figure license:** OPEN: book text + figures CC BY-SA 4.0; code modified-MIT. Figures reusable/adaptable with attribution + share-alike. The most derivative-friendly source here.

## 2. Understanding Deep Learning (UDL)
- **Authors:** Simon J.D. Prince
- **Free access:** Free full PDF at udlbook.github.io; notebooks/slides/editable figure-source at github.com/udlbook/udlbook
- **Figure license:** Book CC BY-NC-ND 4.0; author separately publishes all figures + editable slide source for non-commercial instructor reuse with attribution. No commercial derivatives.

## 3. The Little Book of Deep Learning
- **Authors:** Francois Fleuret
- **Free access:** Free phone-formatted PDF at fleuret.org/public/lbdl.pdf
- **Figure license:** Non-commercial CC (BY-NC style) for the PDF; figures NOT separately CC-released for derivative redistribution. Concepts only.

## 4. Deep Learning
- **Authors:** Ian Goodfellow, Yoshua Bengio, Aaron Courville
- **Free access:** Free to read online (HTML, per-chapter) at deeplearningbook.org; no official PDF
- **Figure license:** READ-ONLY. Figures (c) MIT Press, NOT openly licensed; reuse needs permission. Rebuild concepts independently.

## 5. Neural Networks and Deep Learning
- **Authors:** Michael Nielsen
- **Free access:** Free online HTML book at neuralnetworksanddeeplearning.com
- **Figure license:** OPEN(ish): CC BY-NC 3.0. Figures reusable non-commercially with attribution.

## 6. Deep Learning: Foundations and Concepts
- **Authors:** Christopher M. Bishop, Hugh Bishop
- **Free access:** Free-to-read online edition at bishopbook.com; purchasable PDF/hardback exist
- **Figure license:** READ-ONLY. Figures (c) Springer, NOT CC. Reuse needs permission; rebuild concepts.

## 7. Probabilistic Machine Learning: An Introduction (book 1) & Advanced Topics (book 2)
- **Authors:** Kevin P. Murphy
- **Free access:** Free draft PDFs at probml.github.io/pml-book; also MIT Press (2022/2023)
- **Figure license:** Book PDFs CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 (no derivatives), BUT figure-generating code in probml/pyprobml is MIT, so figures can be regenerated freely.

## 8. Information Theory, Inference, and Learning Algorithms
- **Authors:** David J.C. MacKay
- **Free access:** Free full PDF (on-screen viewing) at inference.org.uk/itila
- **Figure license:** READ-ONLY. (c) Cambridge University Press 2003; on-screen viewing permitted, printing/redistribution restricted. Figures not reusable; concepts are public facts.

## 9. Pattern Recognition and Machine Learning (PRML)
- **Authors:** Christopher M. Bishop
- **Free access:** Free full PDF released by Microsoft Research (microsoft.com/.../Bishop-PRML-2006.pdf)
- **Figure license:** READ-ONLY. Figures (c) Springer (free-redistributed by MSR). Concepts public; redraw rather than copy.

## 10. Bayesian Reasoning and Machine Learning (BRML)
- **Authors:** David Barber
- **Free access:** Free PDF at web4.cs.ucl.ac.uk/staff/D.Barber/textbook; companion cambridge.org/brml
- **Figure license:** Free PDF for personal use ((c) CUP / Barber). No open/CC license; rebuild concepts.

## 11. Gaussian Processes for Machine Learning (GPML)
- **Authors:** Carl Edward Rasmussen, Christopher K.I. Williams
- **Free access:** Free full PDF + per-chapter HTML/PDF at gaussianprocess.org/gpml; MIT Press open-access monograph
- **Figure license:** OPEN: MIT Press open access CC BY-NC-ND 4.0; free download, no commercial derivatives.

## 12. The Elements of Statistical Learning (ESL, 2nd ed.)
- **Authors:** Trevor Hastie, Robert Tibshirani, Jerome Friedman
- **Free access:** Free full PDF on authors' Stanford page (hastie.su.domains/ElemStatLearn)
- **Figure license:** READ-ONLY. (c) Springer (Springer permits the free web copy). Not CC; figures free to read only, rebuild concepts.

## 13. An Introduction to Statistical Learning (ISLR 2nd / ISLP)
- **Authors:** Gareth James, Daniela Witten, Trevor Hastie, Robert Tibshirani (ISLP adds Jonathan Taylor)
- **Free access:** Free PDFs of all editions at statlearning.com; Hastie mirror
- **Figure license:** READ-ONLY. (c) authors, 'All Rights Reserved'; personal/non-commercial educational use. Not CC; recreate concepts.

## 14. Foundations of Machine Learning (2nd ed.)
- **Authors:** Mehryar Mohri, Afshin Rostamizadeh, Ameet Talwalkar
- **Free access:** Free full PDF + HTML at cs.nyu.edu/~mohri/mlbook (MIT Press open access)
- **Figure license:** OPEN: CC BY-NC-ND (MIT Press OA). Genuinely open, no derivatives; we build our own renderings.

## 15. Mathematics for Machine Learning (MML)
- **Authors:** Marc Peter Deisenroth, A. Aldo Faisal, Cheng Soon Ong
- **Free access:** Free PDF kept available at mml-book.github.io
- **Figure license:** READ-ONLY. (c) Cambridge University Press; free PDF for personal use, NOT CC (no redistribution/derivatives). Recreate geometric concepts.

## 16. Understanding Machine Learning: From Theory to Algorithms
- **Authors:** Shai Shalev-Shwartz, Shai Ben-David
- **Free access:** Free PDF on author page (cs.huji.ac.il/~shais/UnderstandingMachineLearning); also Internet Archive
- **Figure license:** READ-ONLY. (c) Cambridge University Press; 'Personal use only. Not for distribution.' NOT CC; rebuild concepts.

## 17. Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction (2nd ed.)
- **Authors:** Richard S. Sutton, Andrew G. Barto
- **Free access:** Free full PDF + chapter PDFs + code at incompleteideas.net/book/the-book-2nd.html
- **Figure license:** CC BY-NC-ND 2.0. Free to redistribute non-commercially, but NoDerivs blocks figure modification/reuse - rebuild every diagram from scratch.

## 18. Spinning Up in Deep RL
- **Authors:** Joshua Achiam / OpenAI
- **Free access:** Free HTML docs at spinningup.openai.com; source at github.com/openai/spinningup (PDF build too)
- **Figure license:** OPEN: repository MIT-licensed (code + docs). Reuse permitted with attribution; we rebuild in house style.

## 19. Algorithms for Decision Making
- **Authors:** Mykel J. Kochenderfer, Tim A. Wheeler, Kyle H. Wray
- **Free access:** Free PDF at algorithmsbook.com (MIT Press 2022); Julia code on GitHub
- **Figure license:** CC BY-NC-ND. Redistributable but NoDerivs blocks figure reuse - rebuild concepts.

## 20. Speech and Language Processing (3rd ed. draft)
- **Authors:** Daniel Jurafsky, James H. Martin
- **Free access:** Free combined + per-chapter PDFs and slides at web.stanford.edu/~jurafsky/slp3
- **Figure license:** No formal license. Classroom use permitted; no figure-reuse/CC terms. Effectively (c) authors - rebuild concepts, never copy artwork.

## 21. Natural Language Processing
- **Authors:** Jacob Eisenstein
- **Free access:** Free draft PDF in author's GT course repo (eisenstein-nlp-notes.pdf); published MIT Press 2019
- **Figure license:** Draft CC BY-NC-ND while under MIT Press contract. No derivatives/commercial reuse; rebuild concepts.

## 22. Computer Vision: Algorithms and Applications (2nd ed.)
- **Authors:** Richard Szeliski
- **Free access:** Free electronic PDF (email-gated) + per-chapter drafts at szeliski.org/Book
- **Figure license:** READ-ONLY. (c) author + Springer; author asks to link rather than repost. No CC; recreate concepts.

## 23. Foundations of Computer Vision
- **Authors:** Antonio Torralba, Phillip Isola, William T. Freeman
- **Free access:** Full book free to read online (HTML) at visionbook.mit.edu; MIT Press CC e-book
- **Figure license:** OPEN: CC BY-NC-ND. Shareable with attribution, no derivatives/commercial.

## 24. Graph Representation Learning
- **Authors:** William L. Hamilton
- **Free access:** Free pre-publication PDF (full + per-chapter) at cs.mcgill.ca/~wlh/grl_book
- **Figure license:** READ-ONLY. Copyrights (author + publishers) extend to the draft; no CC. Concepts only.
