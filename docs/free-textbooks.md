# Free / open ML textbooks (reference corpus)

The textbooks the figure-recreation work drew on. Each is genuinely free to read; we rebuilt the
*architectures and concepts* (public facts) in mlatlas's own style and did **not** reuse any book's
figures, so the figure licenses below are informational. "OPEN" = figures are themselves reusably
licensed; "read-only" = free to read but figures are copyrighted (we redraw from scratch).

24 books.

## 1. Dive into Deep Learning (D2L)
- **Authors:** Aston Zhang, Zachary C. Lipton, Mu Li, Alexander J. Smola
- **Free access:** Fully free interactive HTML, per-chapter PDF and notebooks at d2l.ai; source at github.com/d2l-ai/d2l-en
- **Figure license:** OPEN: book text + figures CC BY-SA 4.0; code modified-MIT. Figures reusable/adaptable with attribution + share-alike. The most derivative-friendly source here.

## 2. Understanding Deep Learning (UDL)
- **Authors:** Simon J.D. Prince
- **Free access:** Free full PDF at udlbook.github.io; notebooks/slides/editable figure-source at github.com/udlbook/udlbook
- **Figure license:** Book CC BY-NC-ND 4.0; author separately publishes all figures + editable slide source for non-commercial instructor reuse with attribution. No commercial derivatives.

## 3. The Little Book of Deep Learning
- **Authors:** Francois Fleuret
- **Free access:** Free phone-formatted PDF at fleuret.org/public/lbdl.pdf
- **Figure license:** Non-commercial CC (BY-NC style) for the PDF; figures NOT separately CC-released for derivative redistribution. Concepts only.

## 4. Deep Learning
- **Authors:** Ian Goodfellow, Yoshua Bengio, Aaron Courville
- **Free access:** Free to read online (HTML, per-chapter) at deeplearningbook.org; no official PDF
- **Figure license:** READ-ONLY. Figures (c) MIT Press, NOT openly licensed; reuse needs permission. Rebuild concepts independently.

## 5. Neural Networks and Deep Learning
- **Authors:** Michael Nielsen
- **Free access:** Free online HTML book at neuralnetworksanddeeplearning.com
- **Figure license:** OPEN(ish): CC BY-NC 3.0. Figures reusable non-commercially with attribution.

## 6. Deep Learning: Foundations and Concepts
- **Authors:** Christopher M. Bishop, Hugh Bishop
- **Free access:** Free-to-read online edition at bishopbook.com; purchasable PDF/hardback exist
- **Figure license:** READ-ONLY. Figures (c) Springer, NOT CC. Reuse needs permission; rebuild concepts.

## 7. Probabilistic Machine Learning: An Introduction (book 1) & Advanced Topics (book 2)
- **Authors:** Kevin P. Murphy
- **Free access:** Free draft PDFs at probml.github.io/pml-book; also MIT Press (2022/2023)
- **Figure license:** Book PDFs CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 (no derivatives), BUT figure-generating code in probml/pyprobml is MIT, so figures can be regenerated freely.

## 8. Information Theory, Inference, and Learning Algorithms
- **Authors:** David J.C. MacKay
